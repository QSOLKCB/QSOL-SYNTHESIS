# Glossary

- **Trustworthy transformation**: a transformation where preservation, loss, and introduced artifacts are explicitly tracked.
- **Recovery under transformation**: reconstructing target state/behavior after disturbance or representation changes.
- **Mechanism claim**: claim that two systems share causal physical substrate, not just formal similarity.
- **Evidence dependency**: one claim requires artifacts or receipts produced by another system.
- **Authority partitioning**: deliberate separation of observation, computation, governance, and interpretation authority.
