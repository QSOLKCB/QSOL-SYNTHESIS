# Research Evolution

The public corpus suggests a change in **methodological emphasis**, not a simple quality ladder.

A tentative pattern is:

~~~text
exploratory correspondence
        ↓
deterministic construction
        ↓
provenance and replay
        ↓
authority partitioning
        ↓
falsification and explicit nonclaims
        ↓
preservation / recovery engineering
~~~

QAI-UFT is useful as an exploratory reference point because it openly combines resonance, ternary/sexagesimal encoding, acoustic mapping and symbolic interpretation. Later sonification work increasingly freezes mappings and separates canonical from interpretive receivers.

QEC and UFF make provenance, replay and evidence promotion explicit. SUBSTRATE, ORACLE, NEXUS and CONTROL then divide knowledge, witnessing, reasoning and operation into separate authority surfaces. UFT-ID 3.0, modern UFF, GEO-REASON and COSMO make the limits of inference especially explicit through evidence layers, falsification targets and nonclaims.

This is a synthesis hypothesis. Individual repositories may contain features associated with several stages simultaneously, and historical influence should not be inferred merely from a later similarity.
