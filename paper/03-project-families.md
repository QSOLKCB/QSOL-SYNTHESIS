# Project Families

The corpus is easier to understand by research function than by subject label.

## Foundational and formal frameworks

UFT-ID supplies the clearest abstract vocabulary for state, admissibility, evolution, recovery, observation, transport, information functionals and constraints. COSMO contributes explicit claim classes separating formal, computational, scientific, hypothetical and symbolic statements. RES=RAG formalizes relational stability and couples its theory to deterministic receipt profiles and falsifiability constraints.

These projects ask what may be concluded from a formal system and how that conclusion should be bounded.

## Experimental and simulation laboratories

UFF, GALAXY, QSOLQEC, QSOL-GEO-REASON and QSOL-SEMANTIC-RELAY operate closer to executable experiments. Their objects differ sharply, but all require declared measurement procedures and controls. QSOLQEC is intentionally permissive and keeps dense/reference execution as an oracle for more scalable representations. GALAXY retains direct or prior Barnes-Hut paths while GPU candidates earn parity. GEO-REASON distinguishes synthetic conformance from future empirical hidden-state claims. SEMANTIC-RELAY isolates writer and reader contexts and uses control conditions.

## Observation and representation laboratories

SONIFICATION, E8_MUSIC, SPECTRAL and QSOL-MAP investigate representation directly. Their outputs may be audio, spectra, event documents or visual structures, but the mature contracts make the receiver explicit. Canonical and interpretive paths are often separated.

## Evidence and authority infrastructure

QEC, QSOL-QEC-BRIDGE, SUBSTRATE, ORACLE, NEXUS, CONTROL and LATTICE treat evidence, persistence, replay, context and authority as architecture rather than incidental bookkeeping. The most distinctive feature is that no single layer is granted automatic semantic authority merely because it stores, verifies, reasons over, or executes something.

## Preservation and portability

PSYCLE-LINUX, RIVET and QSOL-ARK extend the same discipline into software continuity. They ask which behavioural or execution properties must survive changes of platform, implementation or storage substrate.

These families overlap. The overlap is itself informative: sonification projects also have provenance systems; physics projects also have formal claim boundaries; preservation projects use oracle/candidate reasoning. The synthesis therefore treats family labels as navigation, not taxonomy in the biological sense.
