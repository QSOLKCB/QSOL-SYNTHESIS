# Authority Partitioning

Authority partitioning is one of the most distinctive architectural motifs in the later QSOL stack.

SUBSTRATE provides bounded context but is intentionally incomplete. ORACLE records observations, conflicts and unknown states without turning collection into truth. NEXUS reasons across persistent evidence but does not own the evidence or make consensus authoritative. CONTROL orchestrates storage, UI and model interactions but explicitly denies itself scientific truth authority.

The QSOLQEC → QSOL-QEC-BRIDGE → QEC path shows the same idea in promotion governance. Experimental work may mature into a candidate. The bridge may reproduce and validate that candidate. QEC alone decides whether the result joins its canonical lineage.

These separations reduce a common failure mode in AI-assisted research: a component silently gains epistemic authority because it is technically convenient. A search result becomes a fact; a valid hash becomes an authentic claim; a model vote becomes evidence; a successful benchmark becomes a scientific conclusion.

QSOL-SYNTHESIS treats authority boundaries as first-class relationships because they help explain why otherwise different repositories share similar guardrails. The commonality is governance of evidence, not a common scientific mechanism.
