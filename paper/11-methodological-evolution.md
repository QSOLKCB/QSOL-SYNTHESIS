# Methodological Evolution

The corpus suggests a methodological evolution, but it should not be read as a perfectly linear chronology.

Earlier exploratory work such as QAI-UFT openly searches for cross-domain correspondences among resonance, ternary/sexagesimal encodings, symbolic structure and acoustic interpretation. That exploratory instinct remains present in later work, especially in creative sonification. Sources: `src:qai-uft:readme`, `src:sonification:readme`.

What changes is the density of explicit evidence boundaries.

ETQ/SONIFICATION and SPECTRAL increasingly freeze deterministic constructions and make receiver choices reproducible. E8_MUSIC goes further by separating authored interpretive modes from source-forced canonical modes and by distinguishing formal transform properties from implementation and scientific claims. Sources: `src:sonification:readme`, `src:spectral:readme`, `src:e8-music:readme`.

QEC, UFF and the wider receipt/replay architecture make provenance and promotion rules part of the system. SUBSTRATE, ORACLE, NEXUS and CONTROL then separate different kinds of authority: knowledge, witnessing, reasoning and operation. Sources: `src:qec:readme`, `src:uff:readme`, `src:qsol-substrate:readme`, `src:qsol-oracle:readme`, `src:qsol-nexus:readme`, `src:qsol-control:readme`.

UFT-ID 3.0, modern UFF, GEO-REASON and COSMO make nonclaims especially explicit. They do not merely say what a method computes; they classify what that computation cannot establish. Sources: `src:uft-id-3-0:readme`, `src:uff:readme`, `src:qsol-geo-reason:readme`, `src:cosmo:readme`.

A useful description of the evolution is therefore:

EXPLORATION → DETERMINISTIC CONSTRUCTION → PROVENANCE/REPLAY → AUTHORITY PARTITIONING → FALSIFICATION/NONCLAIMS

Preservation engineering then demonstrates that the same habits can be applied to historical software and constrained platforms.

This sequence is a synthesis hypothesis supported by selected public artifacts, not a claim that every project passed through the stages in order or that earlier work lacked rigor.
