# QSOLQEC

**Repository:** [QSOLKCB/QSOLQEC](https://github.com/QSOLKCB/QSOLQEC)

## Purpose
Modular experimental compute laboratory for classical quantum/qudit emulation, diagnostics, and decoder research.

## Documented research question

> How much quantum computation can be reproduced, compressed, decoded, or otherwise made tractable by deterministic classical computation before the classical representation hits the exponential wall?
